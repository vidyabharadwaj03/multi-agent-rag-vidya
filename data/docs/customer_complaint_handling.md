# Customer Complaint Handling Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how customer complaints are received, tracked, and resolved
to ensure a consistent and fair experience.

## Intake

Complaints can be submitted through the support portal, email, or phone. Every
complaint is logged in the support ticketing system within one business hour of
receipt, with a unique ticket ID assigned for tracking.

## Severity Levels

Complaints are triaged into three severity levels:

- **Critical**: service outage or data loss affecting the customer. Target first
  response time is 30 minutes, resolution target is 4 hours.
- **High**: significant functionality impaired but a workaround exists. Target
  first response time is 4 hours, resolution target is 2 business days.
- **Standard**: general questions, minor bugs, or feature requests. Target first
  response time is 1 business day, resolution target is 5 business days.

## Resolution Process

The assigned support representative investigates the issue, communicates status
updates to the customer at each severity-level interval, and documents the root
cause and resolution in the ticket. If a complaint cannot be resolved within the
target window, it is escalated to a team lead, who takes ownership of customer
communication.

## Escalation

Complaints involving billing disputes, legal concerns, or repeated unresolved
issues are escalated to the Customer Success management team. Escalated
complaints require a written resolution summary sent to the customer.

## Follow-Up

After resolution, customers receive a satisfaction survey. Complaints rated as
unsatisfactory are reviewed in the weekly Customer Success meeting to identify
process improvements.
