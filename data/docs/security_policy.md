# Information Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes the minimum security requirements for protecting company
data, systems, and customer information. It applies to all employees, contractors,
and third parties with access to company resources.

## Access Control

All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) for access to internal
systems, email, and cloud infrastructure. Access to production systems is granted
on a least-privilege basis and reviewed quarterly. Accounts are automatically
disabled after 90 days of inactivity.

## Password Requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and unique to each system. Password
reuse across the last 10 passwords is prohibited. A company-approved password
manager is provided to all employees and its use is required for storing
credentials.

## Data Classification

Data is classified into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Confidential.
Confidential data, including customer personal information and financial records,
must be encrypted at rest and in transit using AES-256 or equivalent. Confidential
data may not be stored on personal devices or removable media.

## Incident Response

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Team within one
hour of discovery. The Security Team follows a documented incident response
runbook covering containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident review.
All incidents affecting customer data trigger mandatory notification procedures
in line with applicable regulations.

## Vendor Security

Third-party vendors with access to company data must complete a security
questionnaire and sign a data processing agreement before onboarding. Vendor
access is reviewed annually.

## Training

All employees complete mandatory security awareness training during onboarding
and annually thereafter. Training covers phishing recognition, secure handling of
confidential data, and incident reporting procedures.
