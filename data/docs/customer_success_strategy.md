# Customer Success Strategy

## Purpose

This document outlines how the Customer Success team drives retention, adoption,
and expansion across the customer base.

## Onboarding

New customers are assigned a Customer Success Manager (CSM) within 24 hours of
signing. The CSM leads a structured onboarding program over the first 30 days,
covering product setup, training sessions, and a defined success plan with
measurable goals.

## Health Scoring

Each account receives a health score based on product usage frequency, feature
adoption breadth, support ticket volume, and survey sentiment. Accounts scoring
below the "at risk" threshold are flagged for proactive outreach by the CSM.

## Retention Playbooks

CSMs follow documented playbooks for common risk signals: declining usage
triggers a re-engagement call within 3 business days; a low satisfaction survey
score triggers a root-cause conversation with the account's executive sponsor;
approaching contract renewal triggers a value-review meeting 90 days before the
renewal date.

## Expansion

CSMs identify expansion opportunities during quarterly business reviews by
mapping the customer's growth plans to additional product capabilities. Expansion
opportunities are handed to the sales team with account context and usage data.

## Metrics

The team tracks net revenue retention, logo retention, time-to-first-value, and
customer satisfaction (CSAT) scores. These metrics are reviewed monthly against
targets set at the start of each fiscal year, and low-performing segments are
analyzed to inform policy or product changes.
