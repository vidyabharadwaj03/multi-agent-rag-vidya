# Employee Handbook: Work Policies

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to four days per week, subject to manager
approval. Fully remote arrangements are evaluated on a role-by-role basis by HR
and the employee's manager.

## Working Hours

Core collaboration hours are 10am to 3pm in the employee's local time zone.
Outside of core hours, employees may structure their schedule flexibly as long as
they meet their commitments and attend required meetings.

## Paid Time Off

Employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, plus 10 paid holidays.
PTO requests should be submitted through the HR system at least two weeks in
advance for planned absences. There is no formal cap on PTO carryover, but
employees are encouraged to use their PTO within the calendar year.

## Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews occur twice a year, in June and December. Reviews
include self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback. Compensation
adjustments and promotions are considered during the December review cycle.

## Employee Satisfaction

The company runs an anonymous employee satisfaction survey each quarter, covering
engagement, management effectiveness, work-life balance, and career growth.
Results are shared company-wide, and each department creates an action plan to
address the lowest-scoring areas. Survey participation and satisfaction trends
are reviewed by leadership at the quarterly all-hands meeting.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget for courses, conferences, and
certifications, which must be pre-approved by their manager. The company also
runs an internal mentorship program that pairs employees with senior staff
outside their immediate team.
